OUR STORY

From One Local Idea to a World of Journeys

LankaVista Travels was founded in 2016 by a small team of travel enthusiasts who wanted to make international travel simpler, friendlier and more personal.

We began by arranging customised Sri Lankan holidays and gradually expanded into regional and international travel. Today, our services cover leisure tours, honeymoon trips, family holidays, adventure travel, hotel stays, airport transfers and travel support.

Our approach is simple: listen carefully, plan responsibly and make every traveller feel looked after.
